1. Current Digital Landscape in Kashmir Horticulture

Over 700,000 apple and fruit growers in the Kashmir Valley currently depend on non-interactive digital references:

  • SKUAST-K Annual Extension PDFs: Static 10–15 page PDF manuals. Difficult to read on smartphone screens in field conditions; lacks live weather rules or product matching.
  • Govt & KVK WhatsApp Broadcasts: Fragmented text messages and voice notes. No searchability, historical tracking, or automated chemical dosage calculation.
  • Pan-India Agri E-Commerce Apps (AgroStar, DeHaat, BigHaat): Built for mainland India field crops (wheat, paddy, cotton). Lack Kashmir microclimate rules, zero SKUAST-K stage compliance, and no Urdu interface.
