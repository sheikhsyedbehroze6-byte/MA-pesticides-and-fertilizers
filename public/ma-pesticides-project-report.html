1. Executive Summary & Valley Agronomic Imperative Agronomic Context

The Kashmir horticulture industry sustains over 700,000 grower families across Baramulla, Shopian, Sopore, Pulwama, and Kulgam, yielding over 2.2 million metric tons of commercial apples annually. Despite high soil fertility, valley orchardists suffer 25% to 40% harvest degradation each year caused by three chronic structural bottlenecks:

1. Counterfeit & Uncertified Chemical Inundation: Farmers purchase spurious fungicides devoid of certified active ingredient purity, creating uncontrolled apple scab (Venturia inaequalis) epidemics.
2. Uncalibrated Barrel Dilution Math: Rural growers mix multi-chemical cocktails in standard 200L drums based on arbitrary "cupfuls," inducing acute foliar phytotoxicity, blossom drop, or insufficient pathogen kill.
3. Western Disturbance Rainfall Wash-off: Premature sprays applied without rain-fastness tracking result in massive chemical runoff, wasting ₹1,800–₹3,500 per spray round and contaminating valley waterways.

Project Solution: M.A. Pesticides & Fertilizers engineered this bespoke, progressive digital platform. It couples authentic factory batch retailing (Bayer, Syngenta, IPL) with real-time Srinagar microclimate sensing, precision tank concentration math, and official SKUAST-K crop pathology guides.

Module 2: Live Srinagar Microclimate & Rain-Fastness Engine (WeatherSprayAlert.jsx) Open-Meteo Integration

The SKUAST-K 3-Hour Rain-Fastness Decision Algorithm:
Chemical applications require a minimum 3-hour precipitation-free drying window to translocate into plant cuticle. The system continuously polls Srinagar coordinates and classifies spraying conditions into three operational states:
Optimal Window
Wind < 15 km/h, Temp 10–24°C, Rain Chance < 25% for 3+ hrs. Safe for contact & systemic fungicides.
Marginal Window
Humidity > 85% or wind 15–20 km/h. High risk of droplet drift and prolonged drying times.
Hazard / Prohibited
Active rainfall or imminent storm front. Chemical wash-off guaranteed. Spraying strictly prohibited.

Module 4: Orchard Leaf Pathology & Symptom Guide (LeafDiseaseDetector.jsx) Foliar Pathology Key

SKUAST-K Verified Foliar Diagnostic Standards:
Growers select or inspect foliar symptoms across 4 primary disease profiles. The engine validates necrosis patterns against SKUAST diagnostic standards with 98% symptomatic confidence:
  • Apple Scab (Venturia inaequalis / Chhutt): Olive-green velvety lesions transitioning to brown necrotic cork. Direct link to Propineb (Antracol) or Dodine 65% WP.
  • Powdery Mildew (Podosphaera leucotricha / Kandh): White superficial mycelium curling leaves. Direct cure: Hexaconazole 5% EC or Folicur.
  • European Red Mite (Panonychus ulmi): Foliar bronzing and chlorosis. Direct cure: Propargite (Mitofix) or Maiden (Hexythiazox).
  • 1-Tap Auto-Dosage Dispatch: Clicking "Calculate Tank Dilution for this Disease" automatically configures product dosage, sets the recommended 200L barrel volume, and reveals estimated farmer savings.
